# Data (all SAMPLE data, clearly labelled)

- `suppliers` – modelled on the Central Supplier Database (CSD): name, registration no., directors, date registered, tax status
- `officials` – sample municipal officials and family members (for connected-supplier checks)
- `prices` – real retail prices for 15–20 common items
- `budget_rules` – per-payment cap, weekly cap, tender threshold, categories
